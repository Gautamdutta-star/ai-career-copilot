# 🚀 AI Career Copilot – Engineer Your First LLM Product

An AI-powered Career Copilot that uses Google Gemini AI to analyze resumes against job descriptions and provide personalized career insights, ATS compatibility scores, and actionable improvement suggestions.

## 🌟 Features

- **ATS Score Analysis:** Evaluate resume compatibility with a target job description.
- **Resume Strengths:** Identify key strengths and relevant qualifications.
- **Improvement Suggestions:** Get actionable recommendations to improve your resume.
- **Job Description Matching:** Compare resume content with job requirements.
- **Skill Gap Identification:** Identify missing or underrepresented skills.
- **AI-Powered Insights:** Generate personalized feedback using Google Gemini AI.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Simple and responsive interface for easy resume analysis.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Open the AI Career Copilot application.
2. Paste your resume into the Resume field.
3. Paste the target job description into the Job Description field.
4. Click the **Analyze Resume** button.
5. The application sends the information to Gemini AI for analysis.
6. Review your ATS score, strengths, improvement areas, and personalized recommendations.
